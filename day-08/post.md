Day 8 of building Blockchain 🛠️

Today I built: my own token, Himal Token (HIM), and a faucet that gives anyone 100 HIM once every 24 hours. There are exactly 1,000,000 HIM, and no one can ever make more.

How it works:
Think of free tea at a temple: one cup per person per day. But on a blockchain a new address costs nothing, so one person can come back in a new "hat" again and again. So the temple also makes only 10 pots a day. When they are empty, everyone comes back tomorrow.

What I learned:
An address is not a person. A rule like "once per address" stops nobody. The daily budget is what keeps the faucet safe: an attacker can take one day's tea, never the whole store.

I tested it: one attacker created 50 brand-new addresses in a single transaction. Only 8 got tokens, and the faucet kept 99% of its tokens.
