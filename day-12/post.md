Day 12 of building Blockchain 🛠️

Today I built: an NFT marketplace. You list an NFT at a price, someone buys it, and the artist automatically gets 5% of every sale, even when it is resold years later.

How it works:
Think of a shop that sells paintings while they still hang in the owner's house. Before every sale, the shop phones the house: "Do you still own it?" Only then does the painting move, and a small cut goes back to the painter.

What I learned:
A listing is a promise from the past. In my naive version, an old listing could sell an NFT the seller had already given away, so the new owner lost it. Now the market checks the owner at the moment of sale, and the old listing simply fails.

30 tests passed, and the tests caught all 16 bugs I planted on purpose.
