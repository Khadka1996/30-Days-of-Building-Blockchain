Day 28 of building Blockchain 🛠️

Today the capstone began: Pramaan (प्रमाण, "proof"), a way to stop fake academic certificates in Nepal.

How it works:
A forger can copy an ink seal. So each board or university publishes a fingerprint of every real certificate on the blockchain. An employer abroad scans the QR code and knows in seconds if the paper is real, changed, or withdrawn. No names or grades go on-chain, only fingerprints.

NEB can publish a whole exam result of 10,000 students in one transaction.

What I learned:
The hard part was the bad day. If a university's key is stolen, the council marks when it started: the thief's fakes stop working, and real degrees the thief "cancelled" become valid again.

I planted 22 security bugs on purpose. The tests caught all 22.
