Day 29 of building Blockchain 🛠️

Today I built the website for Pramaan (प्रमाण), my app against fake academic certificates in Nepal.

How it works:
A board uploads its exam results as a spreadsheet. The browser turns every certificate into a fingerprint and publishes them all in one transaction. Each paper gets a QR code. An employer abroad scans it with a phone and sees VALID, REVOKED or NOT FOUND in seconds. No login, no wallet.

Student names never go to any server. They travel in the part of the link after the #, which browsers keep to themselves.

What I learned:
My contract was correct, but my page was not. It showed the board name written in the QR, so a small college could have sold "NEB" certificates. Now the page only trusts what the blockchain says.

I planted 36 security bugs on purpose. The tests caught all 36.
